    Pick a lake first

    Starting…

    Point at a boat launch or a marker you can see and slide the nudge until it lines up. Keep the phone away from the motor.

    Camera guide

    Hold the phone upright. Likely spots, crew catches and launches float over the water in the direction they are. Expect it to be off by 20–40 m at a few hundred metres — use it to steer, then trust the map.
